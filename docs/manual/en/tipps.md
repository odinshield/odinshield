Small routines that make your daily life in OdinShield noticeably easier. This chapter collects proven methods for maximum efficiency.

## Prioritization: The Golden Rule

Not every vulnerability needs attention today. Follow this path to achieve the greatest security gain per working hour.

<div class="manual-stepper">
    <div class="manual-step">
        <div class="manual-step-number">1</div>
        <h3 class="manual-step-title">Critical Risks (≥120)</h3>
        <div class="manual-step-content">
            Address these occurrences immediately. This is where high severity meets critical infrastructure.
        </div>
    </div>
    <div class="manual-step">
        <div class="manual-step-number">2</div>
        <h3 class="manual-step-title">High Risk + Internet</h3>
        <div class="manual-step-content">
            Focus on exposed systems. Anything accessible from the outside takes precedence over internal networks.
        </div>
    </div>
    <div class="manual-step">
        <div class="manual-step-number">3</div>
        <h3 class="manual-step-title">Due Dates</h3>
        <div class="manual-step-content">
            Keep an eye on the <strong>due date</strong> of the entries you plan yourself – the date entered as the target for remediation. The list does not show it; you see it in the vulnerability's detail view.
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

## Classify Honestly

A handbook tip for better data quality: Do not try to manipulate the classification to make vulnerabilities look "more important". If everything is marked as <strong>Critical</strong>, the list loses its steering function. Trust OdinShield's automatic risk calculation.
